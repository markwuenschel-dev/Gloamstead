# Progression and Endings

_Progression through understanding, restoration, and consequences._

## Progression Philosophy

Progression should be tied to understanding and restoration, not merely power level.

The player progresses by learning what the Heart means, restoring meaningful places, and surviving the consequences of those restorations.

## Progression Sources

- restored structures
- interpreted warnings
- survived night consequences
- cleansed or stabilized areas
- discovered lore fragments
- changes in the Heart's clarity
- restored paths into deeper areas

## Possible Progression Axes

### Heart Clarity

The Veil Heart communicates more clearly over time.

### Sanctuary Restoration

The sanctuary becomes warmer, more stable, and more alive.

### World Access

Restoration opens new paths, reveals hidden places, or stabilizes unsafe areas.

### Night Complexity

As the player restores more, nights become more personal and rule-driven, not just stronger.

### Ending State

The ending reflects what the player understood, restored, sacrificed, or misunderstood.

## Ending Direction

Possible ending dimensions:

- restored the Heart without understanding it
- protected the Heart by overcontrolling it
- balanced restoration and caution
- learned the Heart's true nature
- restored light at a cost
- silenced the dark without healing the world
- coexisted with or transformed the darkness

Do not over-lock endings yet. The key is that endings should reflect interpretation and restoration choices, not just a final boss result.
