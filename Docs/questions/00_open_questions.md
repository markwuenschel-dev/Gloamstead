# Open Design Questions

_Questions still unresolved in the current direction._

## Core Loop Questions

1. What is the exact first-night tutorial warning?
2. What is the first restored structure?
3. What does the first night teach?
4. What does dawn show as payoff?
5. What is the simplest failure state?

## Veil Heart Questions

1. ~~Is `Veil Heart` now approved as the final term?~~ **Decided 2026-06-18:** everyday "the Heart", proper name **"the Gloamheart"**; "Veil Heart"/`VeilHeart` is the code term only. See [../world/02_naming_and_voice_decision.md](../world/02_naming_and_voice_decision.md).
2. Is the Heart benevolent, confused, self-interested, wounded, or unknowable?
3. Can the Heart be wrong, or only hard to understand?
4. Does the Heart choose the player, or does the player awaken it accidentally?
5. What does the Heart ultimately want?

## World Questions

1. What exactly happened to the world?
2. Is the darkness evil, natural, or necessary?
3. Was the Heart part of a larger force?
4. Is restoring light always good?
5. Is the final goal to restore, seal, balance, transform, or understand?

## Restoration Questions

1. How many structure types should the first prototype include?
2. Are structures placed freely, restored at fixed sites, or both?
3. Does the player spend a resource to restore things, or is restoration based on discovery/actions?
4. Can restoring the wrong thing make the next night harder?
5. Can the player undo or alter restorations?

## Combat Questions

1. Is right click block, ward, channel, or cleanse?
2. Are enemies killed, banished, purified, delayed, or understood?
3. Should every enemy be tied to a night rule?
4. Can the player die, and what happens if they do?
5. Is combat optional in some nights?

## Scope Questions

1. Is the target first experience 30 minutes, 2 hours, or 6 hours?
2. Is the game one island/place, multiple zones, or semi-open world?
3. Should there be NPCs at all?
4. Should there be any normal resources, or only restoration states?
5. Should the game have multiple endings in the first release?

## Differentiation Questions

1. What makes this not Drake Hollow?
2. What makes this not Orcs Must Die?
3. What makes this not Valheim?
4. What makes this not a walking sim with combat?
5. What is the one mechanic only Gloamstead owns?
