# Open Design Questions

_Current question log. Keep answers precise and mark whether they are decided, partial, or unresolved._

## Answered / Current Direction

### What should the first night teach?

**Current answer:** The first night should teach that the player must pay attention to the environment and that daytime restoration plus dusk preparation affects nighttime consequence.

Minimum lesson:

> I did something during the day. I prepared for it at dusk. Night showed me the result.

It should not overload the player with the full adaptive-night system.

### Does the Heart lie or make mistakes?

**Current answer:** No, not for the current version. The Heart is hard to understand, not wrong.

Reason: making the Heart wrong would create additional systems around unreliable guidance, trust, deception, alternate truth paths, and player verification. That is likely too expensive before the core loop is proven.

### How does the player encounter the Heart?

**Current answer:** The player accidentally awakens it.

### What is the Heart's current personality state?

**Current answer:** Confused, wounded, partially broken, dependent on the player, and increasingly lucid as restoration progresses.

### Are restorations fixed or freeform?

**Current answer:** Primarily fixed-location restorations.

Reason: fixed locations keep restoration tied to place, history, ritual meaning, environmental clues, and fair night consequences.

### Can the player alter or undo restorations?

**Current answer:** Yes. Altering or undoing restorations is desirable because it supports experimentation, correction, and consequence.

### Can restoring the wrong thing make the next night harder?

**Current answer:** Yes. This is core to the consequence model, as long as the result is readable and fair.

### Is restoration resource-based or discovery/action-based?

**Current answer:** Both.

Discovery and interpretation should reveal meaningful restoration opportunities. Night success should provide a baseline resource reward so the player has a safety valve if they miss a discovery/puzzle path. That resource should help recovery, not replace learning as the main progression path.

### What is the simplest failure state?

**Current answer:** The Heart becomes corrupted or destroyed, the light fails, and the player dies or is consumed by the dark.

### What is the first MVP scope?

**Current answer:** Approximately 2-6 hours, centered on one small island/place. Semi-open, but scaled down.

## Partially Answered

### Should the tutorial show night adapting to what the player did or did not do?

**Current leaning:** The tutorial should imply responsiveness but not fully explain adaptation.

Recommended rollout:

1. Night 1: action -> preparation -> consequence.
2. Night 2 or 3: neglected clue or wrong restoration makes the night harder.
3. Later: player discovers the darkness responds to patterns of restoration, neglect, alteration, and misunderstanding.

### Does Dawn show payoff?

**Current answer:** Yes, but the exact forms need testing.

Strong options:

- world clarity improvement
- structure state changes
- Heart clarity or emotional response
- resource reward
- access changes
- memory echoes
- consequence scars
- sanctuary warmth/coherence increase

### What happens if everything is restored?

**Current answer:** The ruin/place should awaken into something greater than a base.

Possible forms:

- awakened ruin
- complete sanctuary circuit
- revealed ancient function
- Heart transformation
- final night rule change

### Will the game have multiple endings?

**Current leaning:** Yes for the larger game. MVP does not need full branching unless it is cheap and directly supports the loop.

Potential ending axis: how the player chooses to grow, protect, exploit, restrain, or transform the Heart/light.

### Can the Heart become benevolent or malevolent?

**Current leaning:** Possibly, tied to player decisions and the Heart's relationship to the Gloam. Not solved yet.

Avoid implementing this as a generic morality meter unless proven necessary.

## Unresolved / Needs Ideation

### What is the Heart?

Candidate directions:

- alchemical world-seal
- last seed of a dead sanctuary
- failed cure for the Gloam
- captured Gloam made gentle
- memory engine that restores coherence

### What is Heart alchemy?

Needs definition. It should support restoration, fixed structures, and symbolic consequence without becoming a broad crafting system.

Possible direction: alchemy as the old practice of binding memory, light, matter, and place into ritual structures.

### Where did the Heart come from?

Unresolved. Key options:

- created by old sanctuary builders
- grown from the place itself
- extracted from the Gloam
- discovered after the world broke
- born from a failed restoration ritual

### What is the Gloam?

Unresolved. Strong candidates:

- unremembering
- entropy with intent
- broken night
- rejected shadow of the old world
- failed alchemy

### Is the Heart tied to the Gloam?

Unresolved but promising. The strongest version may be that the Heart is not pure opposition to the Gloam; it may be a transformed, wounded, or partially redeemed piece of the same force.

### How much combat belongs in the MVP?

Not answered yet. Keep combat questions open. Current guardrail remains: combat is simple pressure, not the main skill test.
