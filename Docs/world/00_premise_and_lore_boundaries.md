# Premise and Lore Boundaries

_Current world premise without over-locking unexplored lore._

## Current Premise

The world is bleak, half-dead, and diminished. A darkness, absence, or hostile force has spread through it. The exact origin remains unresolved, but current direction suggests a rift, severance, or ancient wound in reality.

The player finds or awakens the Veil Heart, a mysterious growing source of light in or near ancient ruins. The Heart is tied to the old world and may be the last fragment of something once much larger.

## The Veil Heart

The Veil Heart is a current working/core term used in the latest direction. It functions as:

- protected object
- cryptic guide
- emotional center
- progression anchor
- world-healing source
- mystery character

It should not be reduced to a health bar.

## The Darkness

The darkness should not be simple cartoon evil unless later chosen. It may be:

- absence left by a broken light
- a natural counterforce
- the world's immune response
- corrupted memory
- an old rule trying to reassert itself
- something that does not understand people at all

Keep this unresolved early. Mystery is useful.

## Lore Delivery

Prefer:

- cryptic Heart fragments
- environmental storytelling
- restored places revealing history
- repeated symbolic patterns
- short discoveries
- player inference

Avoid:

- long exposition dumps
- codex-heavy lore dependency
- clear early explanation of everything
- a normal quest-giver structure

## Core Mystery

The player should gradually ask:

- What is the Veil Heart?
- Why does the dark want to silence or reclaim it?
- Is restoring the light healing the world, waking something, or both?
- Can the Heart be trusted?
- What did this place used to be?
- What does the player become by protecting it?
