# World Baseline and Lore Questions

_Current lore decisions, open questions, and usable origin directions._

## Current Known Direction

The world is bleak, damaged, and half-forgotten. The player accidentally awakens the Heart — **the Gloamheart** — which becomes the emotional and mechanical center of the sanctuary. The Heart is confused and wounded. It communicates through fragments that are difficult to understand but not currently intended to be false.

The Gloam is locked as **the unremembering** (see [02_naming_and_voice_decision.md](02_naming_and_voice_decision.md)); the Heart is a **wounded memory-engine** whose tie to the Gloam is deliberately ambiguous to keep endings open.

## Naming Status — LOCKED (2026-06-18)

Naming is locked; see [02_naming_and_voice_decision.md](02_naming_and_voice_decision.md). Everyday term: **the Heart**. Proper / lore name (revealed gradually): **the Gloamheart**. `Veil Heart` / `VeilHeart` survives only as the code/system identifier.

## What Is the Heart? Candidate Directions

### 1. Alchemical World-Seal

The Heart was created through alchemy to bind, filter, or stabilize the Gloam. It was not meant to be a person, but centuries of pressure, memory, and sacrifice made it semi-conscious.

Strengths:

- fits restoration and ritual structures
- makes fixed locations meaningful
- supports alchemy without making the game a crafting sim
- explains why the Heart is powerful but confused

Risk:

- can become generic magical-reactor lore if not emotionalized

### 2. Last Seed of a Dead Sanctuary

The Heart is a living remnant of the original sanctuary, like a seed, ember, or root-core. It is not artificial exactly; it is the last surviving organ of a place that used to be alive.

Strengths:

- strongly supports restoration as awakening a ruin
- makes full restoration emotionally clear
- ties the Heart to place rather than abstract lore

Risk:

- may make the Gloam feel external unless tied carefully

### 3. Failed Cure for the Gloam

The Heart was an attempted cure that did not finish. It can heal the world, but it also contains part of what it was made to fight.

Strengths:

- supports benevolent/malevolent growth paths
- explains confusion and instability
- makes player decisions about growth more meaningful

Risk:

- needs careful writing to avoid muddying the Heart's trustworthiness

### 4. Captured Gloam Made Gentle

The Heart is not the opposite of the Gloam. It is a changed, wounded, or domesticated piece of it. Restoration teaches it how to become light instead of hunger.

Strengths:

- directly supports the idea that the Heart may be tied to the Gloam
- creates strong ending possibilities
- makes the Heart morally interesting without making it wrong

Risk:

- could undermine the Heart as sanctuary if revealed too early

### 5. Memory Engine

The Heart is a vessel built to remember the world correctly after reality began to decay. It restores coherence by reasserting remembered patterns through fixed structures.

Strengths:

- perfectly supports the Liminal Memory layer of the art direction
- explains why restoration restores perceptual coherence
- ties warnings to half-broken memory

Risk:

- can become abstract if not grounded in objects, ruins, and rituals

## What Is the Gloam? Candidate Directions

### 1. Entropy With Intent

The Gloam is not merely darkness. It is decay that has learned to want. It erases structure, memory, warmth, and distinction.

### 2. The World's Rejected Shadow

The Gloam is what the old world pushed away, buried, or sacrificed to preserve itself. It returns through neglected places and broken rituals.

### 3. Broken Night

Night itself was damaged. The Gloam is the consequence of a cosmic cycle failing, turning rest, dream, and darkness into hunger.

### 4. Failed Alchemy

The Gloam came from an alchemical attempt to preserve life, stop death, or bind grief. The result was a spreading medium that consumes difference and memory.

### 5. Unremembering

The Gloam is the force that makes places forget what they are. It is why paths vanish, structures lose purpose, and warnings become hard to understand.

## Locked Lore Blend (2026-06-18)

Locked (see [02_naming_and_voice_decision.md](02_naming_and_voice_decision.md)):

> The Gloam is the **unremembering**: a semi-intentional decay that erases meaning, warmth, and the identity of places. The Heart — **the Gloamheart** — is a **wounded memory-engine** (an old alchemical work, or a gentled fragment of the Gloam — left ambiguous) that restores coherence through fixed ritual structures. The Heart is never false; its recall is damaged.

This blend supports:

- restoration as the main action
- fixed-location structures
- dawn payoff
- the Heart being confused but not wrong
- possible benevolent/malevolent growth
- multiple endings later

## Unresolved Lore Questions

_Resolved 2026-06-18: the Heart's nature (wounded memory-engine) and the Gloam (the unremembering) are
locked. The Heart's **origin** and **exact tie** to the Gloam are kept open on purpose — they feed endings
(see [02_naming_and_voice_decision.md](02_naming_and_voice_decision.md))._

- Was the Heart created, grown, captured, discovered, or born from the sanctuary?
- Is the Heart opposed to the Gloam, made from the Gloam, or both? (kept ambiguous by design)
- Did the old world deserve what happened to it?
- Is restoration healing the world or reactivating a dangerous old system?
- Does the player's final choice free, feed, bind, transform, or silence the Heart?
