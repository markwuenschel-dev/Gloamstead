# Veil Heart Character

_The Heart as guide, protected object, and mystery._

## Role

The Heart (proper name **the Gloamheart**, locked 2026-06-18 — see [02_naming_and_voice_decision.md](02_naming_and_voice_decision.md)) is the central character-like object of Gloamstead. It is not a conventional NPC and not a simple objective marker. Everyday text calls it "the Heart"; the proper name "the Gloamheart" is revealed gradually. ("Veil Heart" / `VeilHeart` survives only as the code/system identifier.)

It should feel:

- wounded
- confused
- ancient and young at the same time
- powerful but unclear
- dependent on the player
- partially broken
- increasingly lucid as restoration progresses

## Communication Style

The Heart speaks in fragments. Early messages are poetic, incomplete, and difficult to interpret. Later messages become clearer as the world heals and the player learns its language.

Early examples:

- "The ash remembers water."
- "Three lights make a door. Four make a wound."
- "The root dreams beneath stone."
- "Do not trust the door that opens inward."

These should be clues, not flavor-only text.

## Fair Crypticism Rule

Every cryptic warning should be supported by at least two of the following:

- environmental clue
- prior pattern
- visual reaction from a restored object
- subtle audio cue
- changed enemy behavior
- readable consequence
- post-night feedback

If the player has no reasonable way to infer meaning, the warning is too obscure.

## Progression

As restoration progresses, the Heart may:

- speak more clearly
- reveal more of the past
- react to specific structures
- change its visual state
- alter the sanctuary rules
- provide hints that depend on player history

## Damage and Threat

Damage to the Heart should feel emotional and visible. It may crack, dim, pulse erratically, lose motes, or make nearby restored structures falter.

If the Heart can fail, failure should be tragic and meaningful, not just a standard health-bar defeat.


## Visual Character Direction

The Heart should be the purest expression of the art direction's hopeful side, but not in a generic magical-object way.

It should feel like:

- a wounded miracle
- living stained glass
- a warm ember inside a cold shrine
- ancient memory trying to become young again
- a partially broken intelligence learning how to speak

Its glow language should combine muted gold, warm amber, and faint teal. The glow should caress nearby surfaces like oil-paint light rather than blasting them with harsh bloom.

As the Heart heals, its visual state should become more coherent: fewer erratic pulses, cleaner internal veins, steadier motes, stronger local warmth, and more stable reflections in nearby restored objects.

Damage should visibly disturb the sanctuary. When the Heart cracks, dims, loses motes, or pulses irregularly, nearby restored structures should briefly falter so the player feels the injury spatially, not only through UI.


## Current Character Truths

Current decisions:

- the player accidentally awakens the Heart
- the Heart is confused and wounded
- the Heart is hard to understand, not currently intended to be wrong
- the Heart's apparent contradiction should usually come from incomplete language, damaged memory, or the player's limited context
- naming **locked**: everyday "the Heart", proper name "the Gloamheart"; "Veil Heart"/`VeilHeart` is the code term only (see [02_naming_and_voice_decision.md](02_naming_and_voice_decision.md))

## Can the Heart Be Wrong?

Current answer: **not for this version.**

The Heart may be cryptic, incomplete, emotionally distorted, or difficult to interpret, but making it truly wrong would add many additional design paths: unreliable guidance, deception logic, trust modeling, alternate truth systems, and more complex consequence handling.

For the current game, the stronger constraint is:

> The Heart is not wrong. The player does not yet understand what it means.

## Benevolent / Malevolent Possibility

A possible long-term direction is that the Heart can grow toward benevolence, malevolence, hunger, mercy, clarity, or deeper entanglement with the Gloam depending on player decisions.

This is not locked for MVP. If used, it should be expressed through:

- restoration choices
- accepted bargains
- treatment of corrupted structures
- whether the player feeds, protects, restrains, or exploits the Heart
- ending variation

Avoid making this a simple morality meter unless a future design pass proves it supports the core loop.
